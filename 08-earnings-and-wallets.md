# Earnings and wallets

You earn **KOM**, Komunidad's token, for helping the community.

## How you earn KOM

| Activity | Shows in your reward history as |
|---|---|
| Asking a question (after it's checked) | Question posted |
| Being the first to answer a question | First to answer |
| Answering a question | Answer reward |
| Validating a question | Validation reward |
| Voting | Vote reward |
| A friend joining with your referral code | Referral reward |
| An advertiser joining with your referral code | Advertiser referral |

You earn **0.5 KOM** for each question once it's approved. For other amounts, see the [FAQ](https://komunidad.world/faq) on the website.

## The Wallet & Earnings page

Click **Earnings** or **Wallets** in the menu. Both open the **Wallet & Earnings** page, which has two tabs:

- **Earnings**: what you've earned, and claiming it
- **Wallets**: the crypto wallets your KOM is paid into

You can also get there from **My dashboard**: click **KOM wallet** or **Claims & history**.

## See your earnings

Open the **Earnings** tab. At the top you'll see your **Total earned**, and three smaller totals:

- **Received**: KOM that's already been sent to your wallet
- **Ready to claim**: KOM you can claim now
- **Pending**: KOM that's still being checked

It also shows which wallet your KOM goes to (**Paid to** and the start of the wallet address), or **No wallet set**.

Below that, **Reward history** lists every reward you've earned, with the date, the amount and its status. Use the filters to show **All**, **Ready**, **Pending** or **Received**.

### Reward statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Earned, but still being checked |
| **Ready to claim** | Approved and ready to claim |
| **Received** | Sent to your wallet |
| **Cancelled** | Taken back, for example if the content was removed |

## Connect a wallet

To receive your KOM, you need an **EVM-compatible crypto wallet** (for example MetaMask).

1. Go to **Wallet & Earnings** and open the **Wallets** tab.
2. Click **Connect new wallet**.
3. Follow the steps: open your wallet app or browser extension, click **Connect wallet**, then approve the request in your wallet.

Connecting a wallet costs nothing. Komunidad never holds your keys. Connecting a wallet only lets you receive KOM at that address.

You can keep earning KOM without a wallet. Connect one when you're ready to claim (see below: claiming isn't open yet).

### Manage your wallets

The **Wallets** tab shows each wallet you've connected, with its type, network and the month you connected it. Your **default** wallet is marked **Default**. It receives every reward.

- **Set as default**: make another wallet the one your KOM is paid into
- **Disconnect**: remove a wallet. You'll be asked to confirm. Click **Keep connected** if you change your mind.

> **Disconnecting your only wallet?** Your earned rewards stay in your account, but they can't be paid out until you connect another wallet. You can reconnect any time.

## Claim your KOM

> **Claiming isn't open yet.** You'll be able to claim KOM once the token is listed. Until then, keep earning: everything you earn stays in your account.

When claiming opens:

1. Make sure you have a default wallet (see above).
2. Go to the **Earnings** tab.
3. Click **Claim** (the button shows how much KOM is ready, for example **Claim 12.50 KOM**).

Your KOM is sent to your default wallet. If nothing is ready yet, the button says **Nothing to claim yet**.

> **No wallet connected?** You'll see a note asking you to connect one first, so your KOM has somewhere to go.
