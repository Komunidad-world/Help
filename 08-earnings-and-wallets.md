# Earnings and wallets

You earn **KOM**, Komunidad's token, for helping the community.

## How you earn KOM

| Activity | Shows in Earnings as |
|---|---|
| Asking a question (after it's checked) | Question posted |
| Answering a question | First answer / Answer |
| Validating content | Validation |
| Voting | Vote |
| A friend joining with your referral code | Referral |
| An advertiser joining with your referral code | Advertiser referral |

You earn **0.5 KOM** for each question once it's approved. For other amounts, see the [FAQ](https://komunidad.world/faq) on the website.

## See your earnings

Go to **Earnings** in the menu. At the top you'll see three totals:

- **Ready to claim**: KOM you can claim now
- **Pending**: KOM that's being checked and isn't ready yet
- **Claimed**: KOM you've already claimed

Below that is a list of every reward. Use the filters to show **All**, or only one status. Click **Load more** to see older rewards.

### Reward statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Earned, but still being checked |
| **Ready to claim** | Approved and ready to claim |
| **Claimed** | Sent to your wallet |
| **Reversed** | Taken back, for example if the content was removed. A reason may be shown. |

## Connect a wallet

To receive your KOM, you need an **EVM-compatible crypto wallet** (for example MetaMask).

1. Go to **Wallets** in the menu.
2. Click **Connect a wallet**.
3. Choose your wallet and approve the connection in your wallet app.

Komunidad never holds your keys. Connecting a wallet only lets you claim KOM to that address.

You can keep earning KOM without a wallet. Connect one when you're ready to claim (see below: claiming opens after the token is listed).

### Manage your wallets

On the **Wallets** page you can:

- **Make default**: choose which wallet your claims go to
- **Copy address**
- **Disconnect** a wallet. Your earned rewards stay in your account, and you can reconnect any time.

## Claim your KOM

> **Claiming isn't open yet.** You can claim KOM after the token is listed. Until then, keep earning: everything you earn stays in your account.

When claiming opens:

1. Go to **Earnings**.
2. Click **Claim**. If you have a default wallet, the button shows the start of its address.
3. Enter the **Amount (KOM)** you want to claim.
4. Click **Claim**.

Your claims are listed under **Withdrawal history**.

> **No wallet connected?** You'll see a note when you click Claim. Connect a wallet on the **Wallets** page first, so your KOM has somewhere to go.
