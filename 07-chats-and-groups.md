# Chats and groups

Send private messages to your friends, or chat with several people in a group.

Go to **Chats** in the menu. A number next to **Chats** shows how many unread messages you have.

Use the tabs at the top to show **All** chats, **Unread** chats, **Groups** or **Archived** chats.

## Start a chat

1. Go to **Chats**.
2. Use **Search chats and people** to find a friend.
3. Click their name and type your message.

You can only chat with people who are your friends. See [Friends and following](06-friends-and-following.md) to add friends.

## Create a group

1. Go to **Chats** and choose **New group**.
2. Enter a **Group name**, and a **Description** if you like.
3. Click **Add friends** and choose at least one friend.
4. Click **Create group**.

## Manage a group

Open the group and click **Group info**. From there you can:

- **Edit group info**: change the name or description, then click **Save changes**
- **Add members**: choose friends, then click **Add to group**
- **Make admin**: let another member help manage the group
- **Leave group**: you'll stop receiving its messages

## Organise your chats

Open **Chat options** on any chat to:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pin chat** | Keeps the chat at the top of your list |
| **Mute notifications** | Stops alerts from this chat. You'll still get the messages. |
| **Archive** | Moves the chat out of your main list and into the **Archived** tab. Click **Unarchive** to bring it back. |

You can also reach these from **Profile & settings** → **Preferences** → **Manage pinned, muted and archived chats**.

## Read receipts and online status

In **Profile & settings** → **Preferences**:

- **Read receipts**: lets people see when you've read their messages. If you turn it off, you won't see theirs either.
- **Last seen and online**: shows when you were last active.

## Message notifications

Turn alerts for new messages and group events on or off in **Profile & settings** → **Preferences** → **Notifications**.
