# Chats and groups

Send private messages to your friends, or chat with several people in a group.

Go to **Chats** in the menu. A number next to **Chats** shows how many unread messages you have. Use **Search chats and people** to find a chat.

## Start a chat

1. Click **Create** in the menu and choose **New chat**.
2. Use **Search friends** to find the friend you want.
3. Click their name and type your message.

You can also click **Message** next to a friend on **My dashboard**, or the message button on their profile.

You can only chat with people who are your friends. See [Friends and following](06-friends-and-following.md) to add friends.

## Create a group

1. Click **Create** in the menu and choose **New group**, or click the **New group** button in **Chats**.
2. Enter a **Group name**, and a **Description** if you like.
3. Choose at least one friend to add.
4. Click **Create group**.

## Manage a group

Open the group and click **Group info**. From there you can:

- **Edit group info**: change the name or description, then click **Save changes**
- **Add members**: choose friends, then click **Add to group**
- **Make admin**: let another member help manage the group. Click **Demote** to remove their admin role.
- **Leave group**: you'll stop receiving its messages

## Organise your chats

Open **Chat options** on any chat to:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pin chat** | Keeps the chat at the top of your list. Click **Unpin chat** to undo. |
| **Mute notifications** | Stops alerts from this chat. You'll still get the messages. Click **Unmute notifications** to undo. |
| **Archive** | Hides the chat from your list |

> **Before you archive:** at the moment there's no way to see archived chats on the website. Only archive chats you no longer need.

You can also open your chats from **Profile & settings** → **Preferences** → **Manage chats**.

## Read receipts and online status

In **Profile & settings** → **Preferences**:

- **Read receipts**: lets people see when you've read their messages. If you turn it off, you won't see theirs either.
- **Last seen and online**: shows when you were last active.

## Message notifications

Turn alerts for new messages and group events on or off in **Profile & settings** → **Notifications**.
