# Answering and voting

Know the answer? Share it and earn KOM.

## Answer a question

1. Open a question from **Home** (Explore), **Search** or a notification.
2. Click **Write answer**, or scroll to the **Your answer** box.
3. Type your answer and click **Post answer**. You'll see **Your answer has been submitted!**

Not logged in? You'll see **Sign up to answer** instead. Join or log in first.

Your draft is saved automatically in your browser while you type. If you leave the page by accident, it should still be there when you come back on the same device.

### You can't answer if:

- **It's your own question.** You'll see "You asked this question. Answers will appear here as people reply."
- **You've already answered it.** You can answer each question once.
- **The question is closed.**

## Writing a good answer

Helpful answers earn KOM. Click **Guide** next to the answer box to see these three guidelines at any time:

1. **Be clear and relevant.** Answer the question directly and stay focused on what was asked.
2. **Share what you know.** Give useful information from your knowledge, experience or expertise. Avoid guessing when you're not sure.
3. **Add value.** Include helpful details, practical examples or context that make your answer more useful to the person asking and to the wider Komunidad.

## Accepted answers

An answer can be marked as **Accepted**. It then appears first, with a ✓ **Accepted answer** label.

## Voting

Votes help the best questions and answers rise to the top in your city.

- **Upvote** questions and answers that are useful.
- **Downvote** content that isn't helpful. You'll be asked to add a short reason, then submit your downvote. Changed your mind? Cancel it before you submit.

You need to be logged in to vote. You can't vote on your own question, and you can't vote on closed questions.

## Sharing

Use **Share question** to send a question to someone, or copy its link.
