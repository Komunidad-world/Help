# Posts and comments

Posts let you share news, tips or updates with the community. You're not limited to asking questions.

## Write a post

1. Click **Create** in the menu and choose **Post**.
2. Add a title (up to 120 characters).
3. Write your post (**Share what you know…**). You can add links.
4. Add photos if you like, up to 10.
5. Click **Publish**.

Your draft is saved until you publish.

## Edit a post

Open your post and choose to edit it. Make your changes, then click **Save**. Edited posts show an **Edited** label.

## Comment on a post

1. Open the post.
2. Type in **Write a comment**.
3. Post it. You'll see **Comment posted**.

You can reply to other people's comments. Use **Show more comments** and **Hide replies** to expand or collapse the conversation.

## Upvote, save and share

- **Upvote** posts you find useful.
- **Save** a post to find it again later. Click the save icon again to remove it.
- **Share post** sends the link to someone, or copies it.

> **Note:** Saved posts are stored on the device and browser you saved them on. They won't appear if you log in somewhere else.

You need to be logged in to comment, upvote or save. If you're not, you'll be asked to log in or join.
