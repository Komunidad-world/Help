# Asking questions

Ask the people in your city anything: where to find something, who to call, what's happening, how to get something done.

## Ask a question

1. Click **Create** at the top of the menu and choose **Question**. (On a phone, tap the **+** button at the bottom of the screen.)
2. Type your question in the box (**What do you want to know?**). You can use up to 300 characters.
3. Check the **Asking in** line. It shows which city your question goes to.
4. Click **Ask**.

You'll see: **Question posted. You earn 0.5 KOM once it's checked.**

> **Which city does my question go to?** Your main city from your profile. To change it, edit your profile (see [Your profile and settings](02-profile-and-settings.md#edit-your-profile)).

## The review process

Every question is checked before it appears publicly. Until then, anyone opening it sees **This question is being reviewed**. Once it's approved:

- It becomes visible to everyone
- People in your city can answer it
- Your 0.5 KOM reward is added to your [earnings](08-earnings-and-wallets.md)

## Tips for a good question

- **Be specific.** "Where can I fix a generator in Wuse 2?" gets better answers than "Generator problem".
- **One question at a time.** Ask separate questions for separate problems.
- **Ask what you really want to know.** Questions that help people in your city are the most useful.

## After you ask

- Find your questions on **My dashboard**, in the **Questions** tab. (Click your name in the menu.)
- You'll get a notification when someone answers.
- You can't answer or vote on your own question. That's for other members.
- When an answer is accepted, it moves to the top with an **Accepted answer** label.

## Closed questions

A closed question no longer accepts new answers or votes. You'll see: **This question is closed and no longer accepts answers.**

## Finding questions

- **Home** (Explore) shows a feed of questions and posts. Use the tabs to switch between **For you**, **Trending**, **Newest**, **Posts** and **Following** (people you follow).
- **Search**: type in the search box at the top of the page. Results are split into tabs: **All**, **Questions**, **Answers**, **Posts** and **People**. You can:
  - Choose a city, or **All cities**
  - Sort by **Best match**, **Newest** or **Most upvoted**

**Search tips:** if you get no results, check the spelling or try fewer words. Try a more general term, like "generator" instead of a brand name.
