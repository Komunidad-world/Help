# Your profile and settings

Go to **Profile & settings** in the menu. The page has five sections:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your public profile and private details |
| **Preferences** | How messaging works for you |
| **Notifications** | What we alert you about |
| **Privacy** | Friend requests and blocked users |
| **Security** | Where you're logged in, and logging out |

## Your profile

The **Profile** section shows:

- **Public profile**: what other members see. This is your name, username, photo, bio, location, second location (if you added one), the month you joined, and your profile link.
- **Private details**: only you can see these. This is your email (with a **Verified** or **Unverified** label) and your phone number.

At the top of the page:

- **Edit profile**: change your details
- **View public**: see your profile the way others see it
- The share button: send your profile link to someone

## Edit your profile

1. Go to **Profile & settings** and click **Edit profile**. You can also click **Edit profile** on **My dashboard**.
2. Change any of these:
   - **Photo**: upload a new profile picture
   - **First name** and **Last name** (both required) and middle name
   - **Username**: 3–30 characters, using only letters, numbers and underscores
   - **Bio**: tell people what you know about
   - **Country** and **City**: choose your country first, then your city
   - Second location
3. Click **Save changes**.

You'll see **Profile updated** when it's saved. Click the back arrow to return to Profile & settings.

To change your email address, contact support.

> **Why does my location matter?** Questions you ask are shown to people in your city, and you'll see questions from people near you.

## Complete your profile

On **My dashboard**, the **Complete profile** box shows the steps you haven't done yet: add your location, write a short bio, publish your first post and invite a friend. Click a step to do it. The box disappears once you've finished them all.

## Preferences, notifications and privacy

Changes save straight away, and you'll see **Preferences updated**.

### Preferences

- **Read receipts**: let people see when you've read their messages. If you turn this off, you won't see when others have read yours either.
- **Last seen and online**: show other people when you were last active.
- **Manage chats**: opens your Chats.

### Notifications

- **New messages**: alerts for direct and group messages. Chats you've muted stay quiet.
- **Group events**: alerts when you're added to or removed from a group.

### Privacy

- **Who can send me friend requests**: choose **Everyone** or **No one**. If you choose No one, the **Add friend** button doesn't appear on your profile.
- **Blocked users**: see and manage the people you've blocked.

### Security

- See the devices logged in to your account, and log any of them out (see [Privacy, security and support](10-privacy-security-support.md#see-where-youre-logged-in)).
- **Log out of this device**.

## Share your profile

1. Go to **Profile & settings** and click the share button, or click the copy icon next to **Profile link**. You can also use the share button on **My dashboard**.
2. Send the link to anyone. Anyone with the link can see your public profile, but not your private details.
