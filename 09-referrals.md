# Inviting friends

Share your referral code. You both earn KOM when friends join Komunidad.

## How it works

1. **Share your code**: send your link to friends and family.
2. **Friends sign up**: they join Komunidad using your link or code.
3. **You earn KOM**: rewards show up in your earnings.

## Find your referral code

Go to **Referrals** in the menu. Your code is shown at the top, with your personal sign-up link underneath.

You also see your code on the welcome screen right after you join.

## Share it

On the **Referrals** page:

- **Copy**: copies your code
- **Share link**: opens your phone's or computer's share options (or copies the link)
- **Send on WhatsApp**: opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message

When a friend opens your link, your code is filled in for them automatically. If they sign up another way, they can click **Have a referral code?** and type it in.

## Track your referrals

The **Referrals** page shows:

- **Earned**: total KOM from referrals
- **Friends joined**: how many people joined with your code
- **Current rate**: how much KOM you get per referral, and how many more referrals you need to reach the next tier
- **Referral rewards**: a list of each friend who joined, with the KOM and its status (see [reward statuses](08-earnings-and-wallets.md#reward-statuses))

Invite more friends to reach higher tiers and earn more per referral.
