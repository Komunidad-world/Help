# Inviting friends

Share your referral code and earn KOM when friends join Komunidad.

## How it works

1. **Share your code**: send it to friends and family.
2. **Friends sign up**: they join Komunidad using your code or link.
3. **You earn KOM**: the reward shows up in your earnings.

## Find your referral code

Click your name in the menu to open **My dashboard**. Your code is shown in the **Invite friends, earn KOM** box. To see more, open the **Referrals** tab under **Activity**.

You also see your code on the welcome screen right after you join.

## Share it

On the **Referrals** tab:

- **Click your code** to copy it. You'll see **Referral code copied**.
- **Share** (or **Share your code**) opens your phone's or computer's share options. If your device can't share, the invite message is copied instead, and you'll see **Invite copied**.
- Use the buttons for **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **X**, **Telegram** or **LinkedIn** to share straight to that app.

The invite message includes your code and a sign-up link. When a friend opens your link, your code is filled in for them automatically. If they sign up another way, they can click **Have a referral code?** and type it in.

## Track your referrals

Each friend who joins with your code appears in the **Referrals** tab, with:

- their name and when they joined (click it to see their profile)
- the KOM you earned for them
- its status, for example **Pending** or **Ready to claim** (see [reward statuses](08-earnings-and-wallets.md#reward-statuses))

Your referral rewards also appear in your **Reward history** on the **Wallet & Earnings** page.
