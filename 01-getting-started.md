# Getting started

## Create an account

Joining Komunidad is free and takes about a minute. There are two ways to sign up.

### Sign up with Google

1. Go to the Komunidad website and choose **Sign up**.
2. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
3. On the **One last step** screen, pick your **Country** and type your **City**.
4. If a friend gave you a referral code, click **Have a referral code?** and enter it.
5. Tick the box to agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
6. Click **Finish and join**.

### Sign up with your email

**Step 1: Verify your email**

1. Go to the Komunidad website and choose **Sign up**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Continue**.
3. We'll send a 6-digit code to your email. Type it into the boxes.

> **Can't find the code?** Check your spam or junk folder. The code expires after 10 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, wait for the timer to finish and click **Resend code**. To use a different email address, click **Change**.

**Step 2: Tell us about you**

Fill in:

- **First name** and **Last name** (required). Click **Add a middle name** if you want to add one.
- **Phone number** (optional)
- **Create a password**. It must have:
  - 8 or more characters
  - Upper and lower case letters
  - At least one number
- **Country** and **City** (required). We use these to show you questions near you.

You can also:

- Click **Add a second location** if you spend time in another city.
- Click **Have a referral code?** if a friend invited you.

Tick the box to agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, then click **Create account**.

**Step 3: You're in!**

You'll see your own **referral code**. Click **Copy** to save it, and share it with friends to earn KOM when they join (see [Inviting friends](09-referrals.md)).

From here you can choose **Ask your first question** or **Explore questions**.

> **Joined from a friend's link?** Their referral code is filled in for you automatically.

## Log in

1. Choose **Log in** on the Komunidad website.
2. Either click **Continue with Google**, or enter your email and password and click **Log in**.

Click the eye icon to show or hide your password while you type.

### "Check your email" when logging in

When you log in on a new device, we may send a 6-digit code to your email to make sure it's really you. Type the code to finish logging in. This code expires after 15 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, click **Resend code** when the timer finishes.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the log in screen, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Send code**.
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code.
4. Type the code, then enter your new password twice.
5. Click **Update password**.

For your safety, changing your password logs you out of all your other devices. Log in again with your new password.

> **"That code has expired"**: reset codes work for 10 minutes. Click **Send a new code** to get another one.

## Verify your email

If your email isn't verified yet, you'll see a banner on your dashboard. Click **Verify email**, then enter the 6-digit code we send you. If it doesn't arrive, click **Didn't receive a code? Resend**.

## Common problems

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This email already has an account." | You've joined before. Click **Log in instead**. If you've forgotten your password, reset it. |
| "That email and password don't match." | Check your email address for typos, and check Caps Lock. Or reset your password. |
| "Enter a valid email address." | Make sure your email looks like `name@example.com`. |
| "Please accept to create your account." | Tick the box to agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. |
| "Google sign-in could not start." | Allow pop-ups for the Komunidad website in your browser, then try again. |
| "Your session expired. Please log in again." | Go back and log in again to get a new code. |
