# Privacy, security and support

## Keep your account safe

- Use a strong password: 8+ characters, upper and lower case letters, and a number.
- Don't share your password or the 6-digit codes we email you. **Komunidad will never ask for them.**
- Komunidad never asks for your wallet's private key or recovery phrase.
- When you log in on a new device, we may email you a code to confirm it's you.

## See where you're logged in

1. Go to **Profile & settings**.
2. Open **Security**. Your devices are listed under **Where you're logged in**.

You'll see each device signed in to your account. The one you're using is labelled **This device**. If you don't recognise a device, click **Log out** next to it, then [change your password](01-getting-started.md#forgot-your-password).

## Log out

Click **Log out** at the bottom of the menu, or **Log out of this device** in **Profile & settings** → **Security**. You'll be asked to confirm.

## Control what others see

| Setting | Where |
|---|---|
| Who can send you friend requests | Profile & settings → Privacy |
| Whether people see you've read their messages | Profile & settings → Preferences → Read receipts |
| Whether people see when you were last online | Profile & settings → Preferences → Last seen and online |
| Blocked users | Profile & settings → Privacy → Blocked users |

Your email and phone number are private. Other members only see your public profile.

## Notifications

Go to **Notifications** in the menu to see updates about your questions, answers, rewards and messages. When there's nothing new, you'll see **You're all caught up**.

## Getting help

1. Go to **Support** in the menu.
2. Under **What's this about?**, choose:
   - Report a bug
   - Request a feature
   - UI/UX feedback
   - Performance issue
   - Something else
3. Write a short **Subject**.
4. In **Details**, explain what happened and what you expected.
5. Click **Send to support**.

Your messages appear under **Your requests**, with their status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Received** | We've got it |
| **In review** | We're looking into it |
| **Approved** | We've accepted the request |
| **Done** | It's been fixed or added |
| **Not planned** | We won't be doing this one |

Replies from the team appear under your request as **Reply from Komunidad**.

Can't log in? Use the **Contact us** page on the website.
